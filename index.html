04 / Research

Research

Thesis · accepted at IEEE NILES 2026

STREAMTRACK: A Streams-and-Tables Architecture for Scalable and Reconfigurable Multi-Camera Object Tracking

Mahmoud Tayee · Mohamed ElHelw · Ahmed Awad

Detections, tracklet updates, and expirations become append-only streams; global identities, camera topology, and runtime configuration become relational tables. Association is a continuous query, so state is inspectable with a SELECT and the camera network can change without a restart.

Measured against a functionally equivalent batch baseline on WTS (4 cameras) and NVIDIA SmartSpaces (9 cameras), on an edge-class machine with a 256 MB JVM heap. Journal manuscript under review at Future Generation Computer Systems.
